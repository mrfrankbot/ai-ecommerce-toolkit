# Customization Guide: Make These Prompts Yours

*How to adapt every prompt in this toolkit to your specific niche, brand, and audience.*

---

## The Customization Framework

Every prompt in this toolkit has variables. But the difference between "pretty good" output and "holy shit, this is perfect" output comes down to how well you fill them in.

Here's how to customize like a pro.

---

## Step 1: Define Your Brand Voice (Once)

Before touching any prompt, write your brand voice down. Save it as a note. Paste it into every prompt.

**Use this template:**

```
Our brand voice is:
- Tone: {e.g., knowledgeable but approachable, like a friend who happens to be an expert}
- We sound like: {e.g., a seasoned photographer who runs a shop, not a corporation}
- We never say: {e.g., "revolutionary," "cutting-edge," "we're passionate about..."}
- If our brand were a person, they'd be: {e.g., the friend you call before making a big purchase}
- Sample sentence in our voice: {write one example sentence}
```

**Why this matters:** Paste this block at the start of any prompt, and the output instantly sounds like you instead of Generic E-Commerce Brand #4,082.

---

## Step 2: Build Your Customer Profile

Same idea — write it once, reuse forever.

```
Our target customer:
- Age range: {e.g., 28-45}
- Occupation: {e.g., enthusiast photographer, small business owner, content creator}
- Budget range: {e.g., $500-$3,000 per purchase}
- Biggest pain point: {e.g., overwhelmed by options, doesn't know what they actually need}
- What they value: {e.g., expert guidance over lowest price}
- Where they hang out online: {e.g., YouTube, Reddit r/photography, Instagram}
- How they talk about products: {paste a real customer review or message}
```

## Step 3: Niche-Specific Adaptations

### If You Sell Fashion/Apparel
- Replace technical specs with fabric, fit, and sizing details
- Emphasize styling context ("pairs with," "wear it to")
- Photography prompts: focus on model diversity and lifestyle settings
- SEO: long-tail keywords around occasion ("wedding guest dress under $100")

### If You Sell Food/Beverage
- Sensory language is everything — taste, texture, aroma, appearance
- Compliance: be careful with health claims in ad copy
- Email sequences: tie to seasons, holidays, and recipes
- Photography: overhead flat-lays, ingredient close-ups, serving suggestions

### If You Sell Tech/Electronics
- Lead with the "so what?" not the spec (the toolkit already does this — lean into it)
- Comparison shopping is intense — use Prompts 1.2 and 7.2 heavily
- Emphasize warranty, support, and compatibility
- Reviews are critical — use Prompt 5.4 aggressively

### If You Sell Home/Lifestyle
- Room context matters — describe where and how it fits into their space
- Photography: in-situ shots are more important than product-on-white
- Pinterest is your best social channel — use Prompt 3.3 heavily
- SEO: target "best [product] for [room/use case]" long-tails

### If You Sell Beauty/Skincare
- Compliance is critical — avoid claims you can't back up
- Before/after framing works exceptionally well (Prompt 6.4)
- Ingredient education builds trust — use Prompt 1.4 for ingredient breakdowns
- UGC is your most powerful social asset — use Prompt 3.5

### If You Sell B2B/Professional Equipment
- Decision-makers need ROI language, not emotional triggers
- LinkedIn content (Prompt 3.8) becomes your primary social channel
- Case studies > testimonials — adapt Prompt 1.5 to tell customer business outcomes
- Longer sales cycles mean your email sequences (Section 2) need more touch points

## Step 5: Build Your Prompt Stack

Some prompts work best in sequence. Here are recommended stacks:

### New Product Launch Stack
1. **Prompt 1.1** → Write the product description
2. **Prompt 4.1 + 4.2** → Optimize title tag and meta description
3. **Prompt 6.3** → Create the shot list
4. **Prompt 2.3** → Write the launch email sequence
5. **Prompt 8.1** → Create ad copy
6. **Prompt 3.1 + 3.4** → Generate social content

### Monthly SEO Refresh Stack
1. **Prompt 4.8** → Run competitor keyword gap analysis
2. **Prompt 4.4** → Create blog post outlines for gap keywords
3. **Prompt 4.3** → Update collection page copy
4. **Prompt 4.6** → Audit and fix alt text

### Customer Retention Stack
1. **Prompt 2.5** → Set up post-purchase sequence
2. **Prompt 5.4** → Review request automation
3. **Prompt 2.4** → Re-engagement for inactive subscribers
4. **Prompt 2.6** → Win-back campaign

---

## Step 6: Iterate and Save Your Winners

The prompts in this toolkit are starting points. When you get output you love:

1. **Save the exact prompt + variables that produced it** — not just the output
2. **Note what you changed** from the original prompt
3. **Build a swipe file** of your best generated copy
4. **Feed winners back to the AI** — "Write something in the style of this: [paste your best output]"

Over time, you'll develop a personalized prompt library that's dialed in to your brand. That's when this toolkit really pays for itself.
