## How to Use Any Prompt

1. **Copy the prompt** from the toolkit
2. **Replace all `{variables}`** with your specific information — the more detail, the better
3. **Paste into your LLM** (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini — any of them work)
4. **Review the output** — it'll be 80% ready. Polish the last 20% with your brand voice
5. **Iterate** — if it's close but not right, tell the AI what to fix instead of starting over

## Pro Tips for Better Results

- **More context = better output.** Don't just say "my product is a camera." Say "a $2,498 full-frame mirrorless camera for enthusiast photographers upgrading from crop sensor."
- **Paste examples of what you like.** Show the AI a product description you admire and say "write in this style."
- **Use follow-up prompts.** "Make it shorter." "Add more urgency." "Remove the cliché in paragraph 2."
- **Save your best outputs.** Build a swipe file of generated copy that worked. Feed it back to the AI next time.
